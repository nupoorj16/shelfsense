What is this?

ShelfSense treats the products you view as a tiny shopping history. Stage 1 casts a wide net using text embeddings (products with similar meaning), "bought together" patterns and recent popularity. Stage 2 is a learning-to-rank model that scores every candidate on 17 signals, such as similarity, popularity, price and your category and brand habits. The match bar on each card is the ranker's score, relative to the other results.
